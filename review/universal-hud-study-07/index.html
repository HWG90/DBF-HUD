What this study knows about weapon settings

Every reading here is illustrative. The current source exposes ammunition and typed reserves, heat, several charge systems, selectable fire modes, selectable RPM, safety, and known ammunition selectors. This study demonstrates how those fields could share one design language; it does not establish support for every weapon or selector.

Missing fields are omitted. Unknown counts use a dash and NO DATA, never a fabricated zero. Detailed variants such as zoom, flashlight state, fuse distance, or a complete list of all selector choices need additional reader contracts before a HUD can report them.
