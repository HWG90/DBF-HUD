Keep your eyes on the fight.

A small, floating ammo display with a little weight behind it. Move over the scene to steer the simulated reticle; the readout follows with a soft, bounded delay.

ANCHOR / SIMULATED
01 / FOLLOW

The aim point leads. A critically damped spring lets the readout catch up without an oscillating tail.

02 / RESTRAINT

Follow strength and maximum travel keep the display close enough to read during abrupt movement.

03 / CLARITY

Heat fills upward: white below 75%, yellow from 75%, red from 86%, and flashing red/yellow from 95%.

MOTION TUNING

Enable, then drag anywhere in the scene. X/Y offsets are relative to the crosshair; positive Y moves up.
